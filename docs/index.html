Mario's brain and voice run on your free Cloudflare relay. Paste its link below. A Claude API key is optional: it is only used if the free brain can't answer.

In ElevenLabs: add the voice you want to My Voices, then tap ⋯ → Copy voice ID. The pitch and cartoon sliders don't change ElevenLabs voices.

Giuseppe speaks English with an Italian accent; Diego is more Italian. Both play through your relay. If the relay can't be reached, Mario uses the phone's own voice.
